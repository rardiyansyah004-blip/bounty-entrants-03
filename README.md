Entry for make.fun **bounty-entrants-03** — "Bring the most entrants to the
bounties you make on make.fun", $1,000 in USDC. The entry is three free
one-line comedy bounties, each $5 across the top three places, each entry a
single line of at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by a house judge
model in private rounds:

1. **Explain Your Job Badly in One Line** — describe your job as wrongly
   as possible, in one line. The funniest wrong description wins.
2. **The Worst Advice in One Line** — the worst advice you can think of,
   in one line. Obviously absurd, never genuinely harmful. The funniest
   terrible advice wins.
3. **Your Pet's One-Line Review of You** — your pet reviews you in one
   line. The funniest review from your pet's point of view wins.

The bounties this entry counts are named in
[`BOUNTY.json`](BOUNTY.json). `main.ts` reads that file, validates it and
prints the entry's bounties.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
